+- Set royalty, program, or licensing fee caps paid by a franchisee/licensee to a franchisor/licensor; these are payment obligations, not restrictions on pricing of goods/services.

+- Require honoring discounts or promotional rates to end customers; these are operational obligations, not limits on a party's discretion to set prices.
